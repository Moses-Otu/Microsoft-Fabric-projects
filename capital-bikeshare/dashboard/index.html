About this report

The figures are worked out on the page, from the daily and hourly rental files, for whatever filters you choose, so every sentence and chart changes with them. Weather and temperature come with the daily file. Station figures come from Capital Bikeshare's public trip records and count every trip in the selected months. Year-on-year comparisons appear when the dates fall in 2012, against the same dates in 2011.

The bike road

Ride through the data. The more people rented bikes, the more bikes are on the road. One bike ≈ 200 rides a day. Blue riders are registered members, orange riders are casual. Dots on the timeline mark key moments; playback pauses on each one to tell the story.
